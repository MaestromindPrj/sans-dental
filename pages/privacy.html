Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

At Sans Dental Studio, we are dedicated to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal, contact, and clinical information. This Privacy Policy details how we handle the information you provide when using our website and scheduling dental services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details when you voluntarily interact with our website or book an appointment. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Appointment Details: Preferred dates, times, and medical preferences you share on our forms.
  • Usage Data: Anonymized analytical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to optimize our website experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to offer premium dental care, specifically to:

  • Process and confirm your dental appointments.
  • Respond to enquiries, feedback, or dental emergencies.
  • Improve website navigation, design, and user experience.
  • Comply with clinical standards, healthcare protocols, and legal requirements.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

We implement appropriate physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to secure the data we collect. Patient records and clinical histories are kept strictly confidential in accordance with medical ethics and standard dental practice regulations.

4. Sharing of Information

Sans Dental Studio does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We will only disclose your information if required by law or to provide emergency care in coordination with medical professionals.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve performance and navigation. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though doing so might affect some website functionalities.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or medical regulations. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our treatment of your information, please reach out to us at sansdentalstudio@gmail.com or call us at +91 9941055547.
