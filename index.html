Frequently Asked Questions

What dental treatments does Sans Dental Studio offer?

Sans Dental Studio provides a wide range of boutique dental treatments, including Cosmetic Dentistry, Ultrasonic Scaling, Laser Dentistry, Orthodontic Care, Root Canal Treatment, Dentures, Periodontal Care, Dental Implants, and Professional Teeth Whitening.

What are the safety measures adopted by Sans Dental Studio?

Sans Dental has adopted world-class measures to ensure the safety of patients. Our trademarked Stringent 4 step sterilisation process is a testimony to our commitment to high standards of safety & sterilisation. We have enhanced our safety protocols under WHO Guidelines in response to the COVID crisis and are now operating with 10X safety protocols. Our Clinic is now deployed with new state of the art equipment- Aerosol Suction & Decontamination Unit (ASDU) & Constant Fumigation Device (CFD) to effectively manage any aerosols or contaminants in the clinic premises. We follow a zero-compromise policy when it comes to safety & hygiene. This ensures a completely sterile & hygienic environment.

How frequently should I visit a dentist?

The frequency of a visit to a dentist depends on the condition of your oral health. It is advised that people must visit a dentist at least once in six months for regular dental check-ups. However, people with dental problems must visit once in three months or as directed by the dentists.
